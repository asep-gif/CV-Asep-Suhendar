Profile

I started as a waiter in a Jakarta hotel restaurant in September 2000 and have not worked outside hospitality since. Over twenty-six years I have held nearly every revenue and control position a hotel has — restaurant floor, F&B cashier, front desk, night audit, income audit, receivable, credit — and I now read the profit and loss statement those positions produce, across twenty-one properties.

Today I direct financial control, budgeting and internal audit for the KAGUM Hotels portfolio and lead sixty-three finance staff. In late 2018 I was placed in charge of Verse Hotel Cirebon, a 116-room three-star property, running it as officer-in-charge General Manager. I also build the systems I work with: KAGUM SmartData, the group's live commercial dashboard, and XenosHMS, a cloud property management system now deployed in operating hotels.

I am looking for a Hotel Manager or General Manager appointment where operational discipline, commercial analytics and genuine credibility with line staff turn into gross operating profit.
